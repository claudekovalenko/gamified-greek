Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your progress is yours.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, coins, which lessons you've done, which words you know, settings) is saved on your device, in the browser's or the app's own storage. Nothing about you is needed to play: no name, no email, no account.

Cloud save

So your progress can follow you to another device, the game also keeps a copy of it on a server (Supabase), filed under a random sync code made on your device, like k7m2-…. The copy is only your game progress. It holds no name, email or contact details, and nothing that identifies you. Anyone with your sync code or sync link can load that progress, so share the link only with your own devices. To stop syncing on a device, tap Stop syncing in Profile; your progress stays on the device.

What it fetches

The words and concepts come from this website, and the fonts from Google Fonts on the web version (the app has them built in). The game sends no analytics and shows no ads.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
