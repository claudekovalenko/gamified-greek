Get the app

The same game everywhere. Pick your device.

🤖 Android

  1. ⬇ Download the app (.apk)
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (it only applies to apps you choose to open).
  3. Tap Install, then Open. The app works offline and picks up new words and concepts whenever you're online.

This version installs straight from this site. To update, download it again: it installs over the old one and keeps your progress.

🍎 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from your Home Screen. It runs full-screen, like an app, and works offline.

An App Store version needs an Apple Developer account. The iPhone project is ready for when there is one.

💻 Computer

  1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or ⋮ → Install).
  3. It opens in its own window. WASD to walk, drag to look, E to talk, H for a hint.

Moving between devices

In the game, open Profile and tap Send the link. Open that link on your other device and your progress comes with you.
