How it plays

Short lessons, lots of walking, and something to do between every stop.

🧙

One person, one lesson

Thirty people line the road. Each teaches a few minutes' worth (five words, or two or three uses) and sends you on to the next.

🪝

Hooks that stick

Every word and every use comes with its memory hook. Stuck? Tap 🪝 for a hint. The Codex keeps every hook on one page.

🎮

Quick games

Word Rain, the Sorting Spell, Sentence Race, match-ups, acrostic ladders, spot-it on real verses and mix-up duels.

😈

Skulch the Riddle-Creep

Fail a lesson and he steals the questions you missed. Hunt him down and beat his riddles to win them back.

🧰

Riddle chests & shades

Chests that open with one right answer, glowing words to collect, and Shades of Forgetting that quiz you on what you're about to forget.

☁️

Your progress, everywhere

Play on your phone, carry on at your computer. Progress saves on the device and follows you with a link.

A look inside

Each region is a place from the book's memory palace: the Roll Call, the Recording Booth, the Weather Station, the Clock Tower.

Chapter worlds

One world per chapter of the book. New ones open as the chapters are added to the study apps.
